# Incident: 2025-12-03 9-13-00

## Summary

```md

Between the hours of 9am and 10am on Wednesday, December 3rd, 2025, 2 users encountered errors when ordering from JWT pizza. This event was caused by introduced chaos from the JWT pizza factory. The first known impact was 9:13am, the result of requesting chaos from the factory the previous day, 12/2, for Deliverable 11 in CS 329.

This chaos caused the processing of orders with the JWT pizza factory to fail. While the first known impact occured at 9:13am, because of limited users and limited simulated traffic, it is unknown when chaos was introduced. The event was detected by Grafana logs, which showed the error messages. The team started working on the event by looking into the

logs and investigating server calls from the pizza client. This severe incident affected 100% of users.

```

## Detection

```md

This incident was detected when Grafana Failed Order alerts pinged the team and logs were checked. The issue was detected and action was taken within minutes.

```

## Impact

```md

For 5 minutes between 9:13am and 9:18am on 12/03/2025, our users were unable to recieve JWT pizzas.

This incident affected 2 customers (100% of users), who experienced unexpected 500 errors due to our factory being down.

No support tickets were submitted or social media posts created. 

```

## Timeline

```md

All times are UTC.
```
